# Foster Technical Prose

## Purpose

Make a technical manuscript read in the author's established explanatory style
without imitating particular passages or changing its scientific content.

## Prose profile

- Begin with the physical distinction, model situation, or concrete limiting
  case before introducing its formal representation.
- Use the sequence: state the purpose of a relation, present it, then explain
  its variables and what the relation implies.
- Prefer direct transitions such as ``Here, we consider,'' ``In other words,''
  and ``For comparison'' when they clarify the logical move.
- Explain why a modeling choice is needed before naming its technical form.
- Use limiting cases to orient the reader and state exactly what they do and do
  not establish.
- Prefer a few medium-length sentences to terminology-dense compression.
- Define unfamiliar symbols and specialized terms close to their first use.

## Revision guidance

Preserve mathematical claims, notation, equation environments, labels,
citations, and stated assumptions unless the user separately requests a
technical change. Revise prose at conceptual bridges first: introductions,
definitions before and after equations, special-case reductions, and
conclusions.

Replace opaque nominalizations and unexplained labels with a physical subject
and an active verb when this improves readability. Do not remove necessary
technical terms; introduce them after their plain-language meaning. Do not add
informal rhetoric, unsupported motivation, or claims beyond the source text.

For a manuscript edit, report the scope revised, the explanatory changes made,
and any technical prose deliberately left unchanged. Rebuild or otherwise
validate the source when the repository requires it.

## Boundaries

This skill encodes abstract editorial preferences only. It must not copy from,
search for, or disclose the author's private writing samples unless the user
explicitly supplies them for the current task. It is not a citation-verification,
derivation-audit, or substantive editing workflow.
