# Foster (2026), apparent non-associative plasticity

Public preprint: <https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7455160>

The finite-deformation free-energy inequality separates the plastic power into
the deviatoric Mandel-type contribution and the plastic-distention
contribution.  Section 3, especially equations (6)--(8), identifies the
deviatoric Mandel-type stress as conjugate to the isochoric true-plastic rate
and the negative compression-positive mean Kirchhoff stress as conjugate to
the logarithmic plastic-distention rate.  Equation (8) is the direct support
for citing this preprint beside Drumheller's mixture dissipation analysis.
