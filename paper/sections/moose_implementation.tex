\section{MOOSE implementation}

The production material is implemented as
\texttt{ADConstrainedSkeletonBiotMaterial}.  Input-deck parameters identify the
constraint residual properties, the implicit state symbols, the solid Jacobian,
the aggregate solid volume fraction, and each registered solid component's
referential accumulation.  Derivative material properties provide
\(\partial R_i/\partial y_j\) and \(\partial R_i/\partial J_s\).  The material
returns \(\bar v_s\), its constrained fixed-\(p_E\) tangent, \(B\),
\(\bar\rho_s\), and the residual norm.

The pressure-path study in this paper is a quadrature-point constitutive
experiment.  A Q2 mesh parameterizes pressure, the equivalent pore-pressure
backbone and P0 enrichment are both zero (drained, nonreacting conditions), and
the local constraints supply the prestressed skeleton Jacobian and intrinsic
specific volume.  Elementwise monomial auxiliary fields are used only to export
material diagnostics.  The production coupled-mechanics verification remains a
separate Q2-displacement/P1+P0-pressure PETSc Jacobian test.

The constrained verification matrix contains analytic tangent checks,
intrinsic-density/specific-volume inverse consistency, 1D/2D/3D small-strain
limits, the \(B\to1\) and \(B\to0\) endpoints, storage and supported undrained
identities, and the coupled AD Jacobian.  The current focused result is 20
passed, zero skipped, and zero failed tests.  Across the shrinking-amplitude
study, the coefficient approaches \(1-K/K_s\) at approximately first order in
strain amplitude.
