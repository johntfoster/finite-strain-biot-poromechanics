\section{Linearization and analytical Mandel solution}
\label{sec:mandel-analytical-solution}

Cheng and Detournay give the plane-strain analytical solution for constant
poroelastic coefficients \citep{chengdetournay1988}.  For the parameters in
\cref{tab:mandel-parameters}, the analytical storage coefficient is obtained by
linearizing the equations solved by the finite-element model.  At
\(J=1\), \(\rho_s=\rho_{s0}\), \(\phi_s=\phi_{s0}\), and \(\bar J=1\), source-free solid
mass gives \(\phi_s=\phi_{s0}\bar J/J\), and hence
\(J(1-\phi_s)=J-\phi_{s0}\bar J\).  Differentiation of this identity,
using the derivative of \eqref{eq:verification-mineral-factors} with \(a^p=1\) and
\(\partial\bar J/\partial p=-(1-K/(\phi_{s0}K_s))/K_s\) at the reference state, gives
\begin{equation}
 \frac{\partial}{\partial t}\left[J(1-\phi_s)\right]
 =B_0\dot J+\left(\frac{\phi_{s0}}{K_s}-\frac{K}{K_s^2}\right)\dot p.
 \label{eq:mandel-linearized-pore-volume-rate}
\end{equation}
Substitution into the exact water balance yields
\begin{equation}
 B_0\dot J
 +\left(
  \frac{\phi_{s0}}{K_s}-\frac{K}{K_s^2}+\frac{\phi_{f0}}{K_f}
 \right)\dot p
 +\frac{1}{\bar\rho_{f0}}
 \nabla_{\mbf X}\mathbin{\cdot}\mbf W_f=0.
 \label{eq:mandel-linearized-water-balance}
\end{equation}
This reduction defines the linearized storage modulus \(M_{\mathrm{lin}}\) for
the analytical comparison, obtained from the solved phase states.  Following
\citet{chengdetournay1988}, the remaining poroelastic parameters are
\begin{subequations}
 \label{eq:mandel-derived-parameters}
 \begin{align}
  \frac{1}{M_{\mathrm{lin}}}
  &=\frac{\phi_{s0}}{K_s}-\frac{K}{K_s^2}+\frac{\phi_{f0}}{K_f},
  \label{eq:mandel-storage-modulus}\\
  K_u&=K+B_0^2M_{\mathrm{lin}},
  \label{eq:mandel-undrained-bulk-modulus}\\
  B_{\mathrm{Sk}}&=\frac{B_0M_{\mathrm{lin}}}{K_u},
  \label{eq:mandel-skempton-coefficient}\\
  \nu&=\frac{3K-2G}{2(3K+G)},
  \qquad
  \nu_u=\frac{3K_u-2G}{2(3K_u+G)},
  \label{eq:mandel-poisson-ratios}\\
  c&=\frac{
   2G(1-\nu)(\nu_u-\nu)\kappa
  }{
   B_0^2(1-\nu_u)(1-2\nu)^2\mu_f
  }.
  \label{eq:mandel-consolidation-coefficient}
 \end{align}
\end{subequations}
Here \(K_u\) is the undrained bulk modulus, \(B_{\mathrm{Sk}}\) is the Skempton
coefficient, \(\nu\) and \(\nu_u\) are the drained and undrained Poisson
ratios, respectively, and \(c\) is the consolidation coefficient.

The storage in \eqref{eq:mandel-storage-modulus} also has the classical
single-mineral Biot form,
\begin{equation}
 \frac{1}{M_{\mathrm{lin}}}
 =\frac{B_0-\phi_{f0}}{K_s}+\frac{\phi_{f0}}{K_f}.
 \label{eq:classical-biot-storage-comparison}
\end{equation}
Thus the matched logarithmic mineral and skeleton laws recover both the
reference coefficient and the classical storage relation
\citep{biotwillis1957}.  The analytical comparison verifies the small-strain
limit of the implemented constitutive equations.

The Cheng--Detournay characteristic equation defines \(\alpha_n\) as its
positive roots \citep{chengdetournay1988}:
\begin{equation}
 \tan\alpha_n
 =\frac{1-\nu}{\nu_u-\nu}\alpha_n.
 \label{eq:mandel-root-equation}
\end{equation}
The corresponding analytical water pressure is
\citep{chengdetournay1988}
\begin{align}
 p(X,t)
 ={}&\frac{2P_0B_{\mathrm{Sk}}(1+\nu_u)}{3}
 \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}
 \frac{
  \sin\alpha_n\cos\left(\alpha_n X/L\right)
  -\sin\alpha_n\cos\alpha_n
 }{
  \alpha_n-\sin\alpha_n\cos\alpha_n
 }
 \notag\\
 &\mathbin{\times}
 \exp\left(-\frac{\alpha_n^2ct}{L^2}\right).
 \label{eq:mandel-pressure-series}
\end{align}
The corresponding displacement fields are \citep{chengdetournay1988}
\begin{subequations}
 \label{eq:mandel-displacement-series}
 \begin{align}
  u_x(X,Y,t)
  ={}&\left[
  \frac{P_0\nu}{2G}
  -\frac{P_0\nu_u}{G}
  \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}
  \frac{\sin\alpha_n\cos\alpha_n}
  {\alpha_n-\sin\alpha_n\cos\alpha_n}
  \exp\left(-\frac{\alpha_n^2ct}{L^2}\right)
  \right]X
  \notag\\
  &\mathbin{+}\frac{P_0L}{G}
  \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}
  \frac{\cos\alpha_n\sin\left(\alpha_n X/L\right)}
  {\alpha_n-\sin\alpha_n\cos\alpha_n}
  \exp\left(-\frac{\alpha_n^2ct}{L^2}\right),
  \label{eq:mandel-horizontal-displacement-series}\\
  u_y(X,Y,t)
  ={}&\left[
  -\frac{P_0(1-\nu)}{2G}
  +\frac{P_0(1-\nu_u)}{G}
  \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}
  \frac{\sin\alpha_n\cos\alpha_n}
  {\alpha_n-\sin\alpha_n\cos\alpha_n}
  \exp\left(-\frac{\alpha_n^2ct}{L^2}\right)
  \right]Y.
  \label{eq:mandel-vertical-displacement-series}
 \end{align}
\end{subequations}
The implementation retains twelve roots.  A comparison with a longer series
checks truncation over the sampled positions and times; the numerical records
include this check separately from the finite-element error.
