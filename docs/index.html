What this repository computes

The work evaluates a finite-deformation Biot coefficient for a body containing one deformable solid and one water phase. Global fields are Q2 displacement, continuous Q1 water pressure, and Q2 solid partial density. A spatial solid mass balance evolves the partial density, a general local constitutive update supplies the intrinsic solid density and solid volume fraction. Matched logarithmic skeleton and mineral stress laws give a scalar mineral solve and a closed-form fixed-pressure Biot coefficient. A general implicit tangent and centered differences independently verify the coefficient.

The three worked examples

1 · Water-filled Mandel benchmark

The plane-strain Mandel consolidation problem solved with the closed-form Biot coefficient, compared against the analytical Cheng–Detournay solution, with a finite-deformation continuation that reports the departure of the Biot coefficient from its reference value.

10+ MOOSE objects 2 input decks 3 scripts

2 · Poroplastic Biot coefficient

An implicit exponential return mapping couples plastic distention, mineral compression and the Biot coefficient. Independent state and derivative checks support the loading-history and pressure-feedback demonstrations.

3 · Castlegate sandstone comparison

A drained-sandstone hydrostatic comparison against published Ingraham et al. (2017) Castlegate data, identifying the pressure-/porosity-dependent drained modulus needed for a quantitative compactive match.

Reproducing everything
