MOOSE implementation catalog

Every object in the minimal MOOSE application (moose_app/), linked to its source file with a short description of what it implements and why it exists. Header (.h) and implementation (.C) links point into the repository.

Materials

  • ADSolidReferenceKinematics Deformation gradient F, Jacobian J, J̇, F⁻¹, and J F⁻¹ from the displacement gradients. Why: the solid-reference kinematics consumed by every pull-back and the effective-stress relations.
  • ADLocalElasticMineralBiotMaterial Matched logarithmic mineral state, density and volume fraction, material rates, and the production closed-form Biot coefficient. Why: supplies the state-only coefficient and residual derivatives for independent verification.
  • ADConstrainedSkeletonBiotMaterial Independently solves the AD-valued local tangent at fixed pressure and referential solid mass. Why: checks the production closed form against general implicit differentiation.
  • ADVolumetricBarotropicSkeletonStressMaterial Pressure-coupled double-prime stress compatible with the matched logarithmic mineral law. Why: supplies the double-prime stress P″.
  • ADReferenceSolidStressMaterial Combines P″ with the pore-pressure contribution to assemble the total first Piola stress. Why: forms the total mixture stress for momentum.
  • ADBinarySolidSpatialMassMaterial Evaluates solid partial density as its reference value divided by the deformation Jacobian. Why: enforces constant referential solid mass at every integration point.
  • ADBiotPressureStorageMaterial Exact single-water mass storage J(1−φs)ρ̄f(p) and a separate continuous-rate diagnostic. Why: the water accumulation without a prescribed storage modulus.
  • ADReferenceBalanceState Supplies total first Piola stress, current and previous reference fluid mass, and Darcy mass flux to the shared balance kernels.
  • ADBiotDarcyReferenceFluxMaterial Pulls the Q1 pressure-gradient Darcy mass flux to the reference configuration. Why: the reference Darcy flux for the water balance.
  • ADImplicitPoroplasticBiotMaterial Current manuscript model: mass-conserving mineral state, implicit exponential flow and yield solve, and active outer AD.
  • ADPlasticStateBiotMaterial Mass and matched mineral residual provider for the fixed-history, fixed-pressure plastic tangent with aᵖ frozen. Why: feeds the general AD tangent for the poroplastic oracle checks.
  • ADPoroplasticBiotCoefficientMaterial Closed-form coefficient evaluated at the mineral root with Jᵉ = J/aᵖ. Why: the closed-form reference for the independent two-state tangent.
  • ADConstantDeformationGradientMaterial Prescribes a homogeneous deformation gradient with optional time ramp, AD axial stretch, and superposed rotation. Why: drives single-element material tests without a displacement solve.

Kernels

  • ReferenceMomentum and ReferenceFluidMass Integrate total stress and the backward difference of complete fluid mass with reference Darcy transport. The kernel source agrees with the anisotropic companion apart from application registration.
  • ADMaterialPropertyResidual Weak algebraic residual using an AD material property. Why: probes returned material quantities in constitutive Jacobian tests. The production mineral and plastic constraints are solved inside materials.

Postprocessors & application

  • ADMaterialScalarL2Error Element-integral L2 error of a material property against an analytical function. Why: reports the Biot-identity, mineral-EOS, and referential-mass diagnostics.
  • NonlinearBiotADApp Application object registering all materials, kernels, and postprocessors. Why: the minimal MOOSE application entry point.
  • main.C Standard MOOSE main entry point. Why: the executable.

Coupled plastic storage and diagnostics

  • ADPoroplasticPoreVolumeMaterial supplies the current solid fraction for conservative water storage and a separate continuous-rate diagnostic.
  • NodalDofSum sums assembled nodal quantities for conservation diagnostics.

Traceability

The mapping from manuscript equations to these MOOSE objects is recorded in equation_to_moose_map.yml, and the theory-to-implementation trace in theory_traceability.yml. The verification index links the current evidence and reproduction commands.

The map covers every manuscript equation identifier and every local material, kernel, and postprocessor. Supporting entries identify derivations, limiting cases, and analytical Mandel formulas separately from equations assembled by MOOSE. Each entry links the relevant source or verification calculation. make validate rejects missing coverage, deleted equation labels, unknown objects, and missing tests.

The shared objects also expose general simulator options, including pressure enrichment, extra stresses, and additional fluxes. Those options are inactive in the paper's input decks. The reported formulation uses continuous Q1 pressure, zero gravity, and the single-water Darcy flux.

Numbered equations and unnumbered displays retain stable source identifiers in the traceability map. Only equations referenced in the manuscript prose carry printed numbers.
