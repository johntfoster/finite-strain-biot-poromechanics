# Licensing and provenance

The original manuscript text, scripts, and input decks in this repository are
working research products. Their publication license is intentionally left to
the authors before public release.

The Lawal--Kim article and the associated Zenodo data release are distributed
under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Their authors, DOIs, release metadata,
and the exact workbook/figure provenance are recorded in
`references/LAWAL_KIM_2026.md`. The source data remain attributed third-party
materials; inclusion here does not change their license or authorship.

The production MOOSE source remains in the separate
`multicomponent_reactive_flow` repository. This repository records its paths and
roles in `moose/source_manifest.yml` rather than copying that source.
