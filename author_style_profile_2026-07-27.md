# Author profile for repository edits

This profile is inherited from the parent `multicomponent_reactive_flow`
repository. Read it before every edit. Explicit instructions and accepted
notation take precedence.

## Characteristic voice

- Begin with the physical system or mathematical task.
- State the central contribution early and positively.
- Move from a familiar continuum-mechanics statement to the required extension;
  give the physical reason before detailed notation.
- Keep derivations equation-forward: purpose, equation, local definitions,
  consequence, and limiting interpretation.
- Use concrete subjects and verbs: the balance gives, the constraint yields,
  substitution produces, and the small-strain limit recovers.
- Distinguish definitions, derived results, constitutive choices, calibrations,
  assumptions, and predictions explicitly.
- Interpret formal results physically and connect them to observable or
  verifiable consequences.

## Exposition and notation

- Use moderate-length technical sentences and one recognizable task per
  paragraph.
- Define notation within one or two paragraphs of first substantive use.
- Do not collect symbols early merely because they will be needed later.
- Do not introduce a named helper quantity when the accepted primitive
  variables state the result clearly.
- Use first-person plural for paper-level actions; within derivations, prefer
  the equation, balance, constraint, or physical quantity as subject.
- Treat equations as grammatical parts of sentences and punctuate them.

## Prior work and contribution

- State affirmatively what a cited source establishes, then state what this
  paper derives, implements, or verifies.
- Do not build novelty by listing what earlier work lacks.
- Use negation only for necessary mathematical conditions, operative
  assumptions, precise distinctions, or limitations preventing
  misinterpretation.
- Do not put drafting history or author-agent discussion into the paper.

## Technical preservation

- Preserve parent-manuscript equations, symbols, labels, citations, and
  constitutive distinctions unless the task explicitly changes them.
- Preserve the difference between intrinsic and bulk density, fixed-pressure
  and total derivatives, inner implicit differentiation and outer AD, and
  calibration and prediction.
- Improve nearby explanation before adding notation.

## Review checklist

Before editing, identify the reader-facing problem, currently defined symbols,
the controlling parent equations, and the smallest compatible change. After
editing, confirm that every symbol is defined, each equation paragraph explains
purpose and consequence, claims are classified correctly, accepted notation is
preserved, and development-history language is absent.
