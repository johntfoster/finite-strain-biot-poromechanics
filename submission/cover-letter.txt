Dear Editors,

Please consider "Pressure coupling and the Biot coefficient in finite-strain
poroelasticity and poroplasticity" for publication in the Journal of the
Mechanics and Physics of Solids.

The manuscript derives pressure coupling from a common stored energy for
mineral compression and permanent pore-volume change. It distinguishes the
reversible Biot coefficient from the multiplier of a finite pressure change,
and implements the coupled mineral and plastic update with automatic
differentiation. Constitutive checks, assembled Jacobian comparisons, the
analytical Mandel benchmark, and refinement studies support the numerical
results. The calculations use synthetic material parameters; experimental
validation remains future work.

The accompanying public repository provides the implementation, input decks,
curated numerical data, verification drivers, figure scripts, and development
history. A versioned release identifies the submission package. The companion
website and configured development container support inspection and
reproduction. The manuscript discloses the use of generative AI tools and
describes the author's responsibility for the work.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. I have no competing
financial interests or personal relationships that could have influenced this
work.
